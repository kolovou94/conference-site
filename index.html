Welcome

About the Conference

The IWA International Conference on Water and Environment: Technology – Sustainability – Culture – Heritage 2028 will take place in the city of Patras, Greece from 21–23 March 2028.

In this conference, scientists, engineers, archaeologists, heritage professionals, and environmental authorities come together to construct a lively dialogue at the intersection of innovation, sustainability, and cultural heritage in water management.

Organised by the Hellenic Open University (H.O.U.) and endorsed by the IWA Specialist Group on Water and Wastewater in Ancient Civilisations, the event aims to highlight the evolution of water technologies and foster collaborations that can inspire future sustainable applications.

  • Water heritage, archaeology and culture
  • Digital transformation and smart water systems
  • Climate change adaptation and resilience
  • Water, health and society
  • Ancient influences on modern technologies
  • Governance, education and community engagement

Authors

Submission Guidelines

📝 Abstract Requirements

  • All abstracts must be submitted in English
  • Maximum 260 words
  • Oral Presentations: 15-minute slot (12 min presentation + 3 min Q&A)
  • Poster Presentations: A0 size, vertical orientation, displayed during coffee breaks

🔢 Submission Limits

  • Maximum of 2 publications or 2 abstracts per single author
  • For collaborative works, at least 2 main authors must register and pay fees if two separate presentations are accepted

📅 Key Deadlines

Extended
  • Abstract Submission: 1 December 2027
  • Full Paper Submission: 15 January 2028

📚 Proceedings

  • Digital Proceedings: All extended abstracts included in the official Conference E-Book of Abstracts, hosted on the congress website and IWA network pages
  • Journal Publication: High-quality papers fast-tracked for peer review in IWA Publishing Journals
25% OA Discount

A 25% Open Access discount is offered to all corresponding authors who are members of the International Water Association. For more information, visit iwaponline.com.

About Patras

Patras, one of Greece's largest university cities, blends cultural heritage with a dynamic academic community and spectacular coastal scenery along the Gulf of Patras.

The city offers a wide selection of hotels, restaurants, and transportation options. Its vibrant student population creates a lively, welcoming atmosphere for international visitors.
